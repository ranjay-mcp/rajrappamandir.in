Visitor Information

Plan your visit to Rajrappa

Rajrappa is a major pilgrimage destination in Jharkhand, located near the confluence of the Damodar and Bhairavi (Bhera) rivers.

📍 LocationRajrappa, Ramgarh District, Jharkhand, India
🙏 Main DeityMaa Chhinnamasta
🌊 LandmarkConfluence of the Damodar and Bhairavi rivers
Before You Visit

Prepare for a peaceful darshan

Festival days and special occasions can attract large numbers of devotees. Allow extra travel and waiting time, and follow the latest instructions at the temple.
