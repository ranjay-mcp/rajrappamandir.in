The Sacred Temple

A place of faith, heritage and divine energy

Rajrappa is home to Maa Chhinnamasta and is one of Jharkhand's best-known pilgrimage destinations. The temple stands at the confluence of the Damodar and Bhairavi (Bhera) rivers.
