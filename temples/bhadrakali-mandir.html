Welcome to Itkhori

A place of devotion and heritage

Maa Bhadrakali Mandir is located in Bhaduli village, around 1.5 km from Itkhori block in Chatra district, Jharkhand. The temple is a major religious destination and part of a wider landscape of sacred sites and archaeological heritage.

The area is notable for the way Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain traditions are represented in its shrines, sculptures, and heritage remains. Visitors can combine prayer and reflection with an appreciation of the region's history and natural surroundings.

This is an independent visitor guide. Please confirm current darshan timings, festival arrangements, and access locally before travelling.
